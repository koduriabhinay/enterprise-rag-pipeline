# Data Retention Policy

Effective March 2025. Owned by the Data Governance team at Northwind Traders.

## Purpose

This policy defines how long business records are kept and how they are securely destroyed. It applies to all electronic and paper records created in the course of business.

## Retention Schedule

Financial records, including invoices, receipts, and tax filings, are retained for 7 years from the end of the fiscal year in which they were created. Customer contracts are retained for 7 years after contract termination. System and application logs are retained for 3 years. Marketing campaign data is retained for 2 years.

## Deletion

When a retention period expires, records are queued for deletion within 30 days. Backups containing expired records are purged on the next backup rotation cycle, at most 90 days later. Deletion of customer personal data follows the privacy request workflow and is completed within 30 days of a verified request.

## Legal Holds

If litigation is reasonably anticipated, the legal team issues a legal hold that suspends all deletion for the affected records. Employees must not delete, alter, or destroy any records covered by a legal hold. Holds are reviewed every 6 months.

## Employee Records

Personnel files are retained for 7 years after an employee's departure. Recruiting records for candidates who were not hired are retained for 3 years. Payroll records follow the 7-year financial retention schedule. Performance reviews are kept for the duration of employment plus 4 years.
