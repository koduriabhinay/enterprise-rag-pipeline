# Remote Work Policy

Effective January 2026. This policy applies to all full-time employees of Northwind Traders.

## Eligibility

Employees are eligible for remote work after completing 90 days of employment with a performance rating of "meets expectations" or higher. Roles that require on-site presence, such as facilities and hardware lab staff, are excluded. Eligibility is reviewed annually by the employee's manager.

## Equipment

The company provides each remote employee with a laptop, a 27-inch monitor, a keyboard, a mouse, and a headset. A one-time home office stipend of $800 is available for furniture. Equipment requests are submitted through the IT service portal and are typically fulfilled within 10 business days.

## Security

Remote employees must connect through the corporate VPN when accessing internal systems. Home Wi-Fi networks must use WPA2 or WPA3 encryption. Company devices must have full-disk encryption enabled and screen lock set to 5 minutes or less. For help, contact the IT service desk at it-helpdesk@example.com or 555-0142.

## Work Hours and Availability

Remote employees are expected to be available during core collaboration hours of 10 AM to 3 PM Central Time. Any recurring schedule change must be approved by the employee's manager two weeks in advance.

## Tax and Compliance

Employees working remotely from a state other than the company's registered states must notify HR within 30 days of relocation. The company withholds taxes based on the employee's declared work location. Remote work from outside the country requires written approval from HR and Legal at least 60 days in advance.
