# Acceptable Use Policy

Effective February 2025. Owned by the Information Security team at Northwind Traders.

## Passwords and Authentication

Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and include uppercase, lowercase, numeric, and special characters. Multi-factor authentication is required for all corporate systems. Passwords must not be reused across corporate and personal accounts, and must be changed immediately if compromise is suspected.

## Devices

Company-issued laptops must have full-disk encryption and endpoint protection installed. Personal devices may access corporate email and chat only after enrolling in mobile device management (MDM). Jailbroken or rooted devices are never permitted to access corporate resources.

## Network and Data

Employees must not download customer data to personal devices or unapproved cloud storage. Public Wi-Fi may only be used with the corporate VPN active. Any suspected malware or phishing attempt must be reported to security within 24 hours.

## Monitoring

The company monitors network traffic and device compliance for security purposes. Employees should have no expectation of privacy for activity conducted on company systems or networks.

## Software

Only approved software from the corporate catalog may be installed on company devices. Open-source tools require security review before use with customer data. Browser extensions are limited to an approved list maintained by the security team. Unlicensed software is prohibited on all company systems.
