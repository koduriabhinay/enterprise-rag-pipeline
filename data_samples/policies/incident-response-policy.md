# Incident Response Policy

Effective June 2025. Owned by the Security Operations team at Northwind Traders.

## Severity Levels

SEV1 (Critical): complete outage of a customer-facing service or a confirmed data breach. SEV2 (Major): significant degradation affecting more than 20 percent of users. SEV3 (Minor): limited impact affecting a small subset of users. SEV4 (Low): cosmetic issues or single-user problems with a workaround available.

## Response Times

A SEV1 incident requires acknowledgement within 15 minutes and an incident commander assigned within 30 minutes. SEV2 incidents require acknowledgement within 1 hour. SEV3 incidents require acknowledgement within 4 business hours. SEV4 incidents are handled through the normal support queue.

## Breach Notification

Any suspected data breach must be reported to the security team within 24 hours of discovery. If a breach is confirmed, affected customers are notified within 72 hours, in line with regulatory requirements. A post-incident review is conducted within 5 business days of resolution for all SEV1 and SEV2 incidents.

## Communication

Status updates for SEV1 incidents are posted every 30 minutes until resolution. SEV2 updates are posted every 2 hours. All incident communication uses the dedicated incident channel, not direct messages, so the full timeline is preserved.

## Roles

The incident commander owns coordination and decision making during SEV1 and SEV2 incidents. The communications lead posts status updates and handles customer messaging. The scribe maintains the incident timeline. These roles are assigned at declaration time and may rotate during long-running incidents.
