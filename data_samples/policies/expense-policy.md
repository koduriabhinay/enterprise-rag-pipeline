# Expense Policy

Effective January 2026. Owned by the Finance team at Northwind Traders.

## Per Diem

Employees traveling on company business receive a meal per diem of $75 per day. The per diem covers breakfast, lunch, and dinner; it is reduced by 50 percent on travel days. Alcohol is never reimbursable.

## Approval Thresholds

Expenses under $500 require manager approval. Expenses between $500 and $5,000 require manager and director approval. Any single expense over $5,000 requires vice president approval before the purchase is made. Retroactive approvals are granted only in documented emergencies.

## Submission

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date. Receipts are required for all expenses of $25 or more. Reports submitted after 60 days are reimbursed at 50 percent. Reports older than 90 days are not reimbursed.

## Prohibited Expenses

First-class airfare, personal entertainment, traffic fines, and clothing are not reimbursable. Gift cards purchased as employee gifts require finance pre-approval regardless of amount.

## Travel Booking

Flights and hotels must be booked through the corporate travel portal to qualify for reimbursement. The standard is economy class for flights under 6 hours and premium economy for longer flights. Business class requires vice president approval. Personal side trips attached to business travel are not reimbursable.
